AUTHORS

Meet the writers behind Sinister Books Press, from dark fiction and literary drama to adventures created for young readers.

Author / Founder

Eric Erter

Eric Erter writes across horror, suspense, science fiction, and literary drama, united by characters who feel genuine. He is drawn to the quiet moments between the action—the conversations and private fears that reveal who people are.

Erter began writing in high school, where his poems and short stories were published, and later studied creative writing in college. His background includes comics, screenwriting, and film criticism. For the past twenty years, he has worked as a utility lineman.

His work includes the six-volume Snowblind Series, Before the Streetlights, Animal Hours, The Tides That Bind, Old Money, Goodnight, House, and Operation: Silver Kite. His life as a husband and father of three strongly influences his writing.

Influenced by Stephen King, Neil Gaiman, Michael Connelly, films, comics, and story-driven games, Erter founded Sinister Books for stories that disturb without relying on gore. He lives with his family in Arlington, Texas.

Father-Daughter Writing Team

Eric & Arya Erter

Eric and Arya Erter are a father-daughter writing team behind the Amazing Arya Adventures.

The fictional Arya is inspired by real experiences, family adventures, and the kind of imagination that can turn an ordinary day into something much bigger. The books are built around curiosity, humor, discovery, and a little bit of magic.

Arya enjoys reading, writing, and art, along with ice hockey, figure skating, taekwondo, and gymnastics. She is also a gamer who enjoys Roblox and Minecraft, and her favorite movie is Coraline.

Together, Eric and Arya turn those interests and experiences into colorful adventures designed to make young readers laugh, imagine, and explore.
