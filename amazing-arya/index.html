The Authors

Eric & Arya Erter

Eric and Arya Erter are a father-and-daughter writing team who bring the fictional Arya to life through adventures inspired by both real experiences and imagination.

Together they created Amazing Arya's Adventures to explore the world through curiosity, humor, discovery and a little magic.

Arya loves reading, writing and art as well as ice hockey, figure skating, taekwondo and gymnastics. She also enjoys Roblox and Minecraft, and her favorite movie is Coraline.
