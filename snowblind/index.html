About the Series

Survival Was Only the Beginning

The Snowblind Series begins with an intimate survival story: people trapped by weather, geography and fear, forced to depend on one another when the world outside becomes unreachable.

From there, the story continually widens. What first appears isolated becomes connected. What seems impossible becomes increasingly difficult to dismiss. And the question gradually changes from how people survive to what survival may turn them into.

Across six volumes, the series moves between horror, suspense, science fiction and character drama while remaining centered on the people caught inside it.
