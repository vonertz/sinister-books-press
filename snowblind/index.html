THE SNOWBLIND SERIES

A six-volume saga of survival, loyalty, faith, transformation, and the choices people make when the world they knew no longer exists.

Where It Begins

What begins as a carefree road trip from Minneapolis to Las Vegas for a bachelor party quickly becomes a fight for survival in the mountains.

From there, the story grows far beyond the original group, following the consequences of what happened and the people forced to decide what comes next.
