Reader Q&A

Ask About the Stories

The goal of Ask the Author is not simply to provide private answers. Selected questions can become part of an ongoing public Q&A, allowing readers to explore the stories together.

Questions can be about individual books, characters, influences, writing decisions, the publishing process or projects currently in development.

Spoiler-sensitive answers can be clearly marked so readers can decide whether they want to continue.
